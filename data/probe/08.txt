The NORDEN DNA 01 Tradition meets openness We come from a century-old industry built on trust, responsibility and precision. A heritage we are proud to carry forward. At the same time, our culture is open, social and collaborative. We value honest dialogue, shared ownership and the energy that comes from moving forward together. Our strong social environment is further supported by our active Activity Club. 02 Onboarding that connects you to our culture Alongside a thorough local onboarding to your role, all new colleagues across locations join our New@NORDEN programme in Copenhagen . A three‑day experience that brings our purpose, values and business to life. It is designed to help you understand how we work, collaborate and make decisions, while building strong connections with colleagues across teams and geographies. 03 Culture activated, not just described Our Soulship programme turns values into everyday habits via live training sessions, on-demand tools and ongoing conversations that strengthen feedback, collaboration and growth for employees and managers. 04 Shared leadership language All people managers undergoes training in our tailored leadership framework aimed at defining what good leadership looks like, building consistency in expectations and developing our managers leadership capabilities. 05 Innovating on a strong foundation We value innovation and continually push our digital capabilities forward, pairing a modern, tech‑driven mindset with a strong focus on data, automation and AI to enable smarter decisions and drive meaningful progress. >> EXPLORE norden NORDEN’s Q&A Can I submit an unsolicited application? To comply with European GDPR requirements, we are unable to accept or process applications submitted by email or through other channels. But you are welcome to submit an unsolicited application through our recruitment system . Looking for a career at sea? NORDEN does not employ seafarers. Seafarer employment and recruitment are handled externally through Technical Management by Synergy Marine and InterMaritime.
